# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good faith.
- Give useful, specific feedback.
- Welcome questions from beginners.
- Focus criticism on code, behavior, and technical decisions rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, or intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project constructive.
